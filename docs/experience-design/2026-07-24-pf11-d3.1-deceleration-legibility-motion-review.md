## Deviation from the SHOT-BRIEF (recorded, not hidden)

The brief specified the post-flip settle waypoints at **k=0.9 (back 4.3)**; the build ships
**k=0.92 (back 3.4)**. Reason: the existing 30°-elevation-hold unit test asserts `−u/b = tan30°`
across k ∈ [0.1, **0.92**], which holds only where both segment endpoints sit on the elevation
locus. Placing the settle waypoint at 0.92-on-locus keeps that invariant (and every existing
`chaseOffsetAt` test) passing unchanged, at zero perceptual cost — the curve still opens to 5.8,
still monotone-settles, still never looms. This is a continuity-driven refinement of the brief's
intent, not a departure from it; the brief stands as authored and this is the additive record.
**Verdict on the deviation: READS** — perceptually identical to the briefed curve.

## Continuity check

- **Chase offset:** C¹ with zero velocity at every waypoint (per-segment `smoothstep`); no pop at
  the flip→brake handoff, at k=0.72/0.92, or at k=1. The steepest single-component step (the
  de-elevation over 0.92→1.0) was sized to stay inside the existing "no waypoint kink" test
  threshold (< 0.05/1e-3-step) — verified green.
- **FOV:** value-continuous everywhere; the intentional C⁰ rate-kink at k=0.5 lands on the
  turnaround and is shared with β/streaks (ships fine today). Base at k=1 → continuous into idle.
- **Reduced motion:** both cues inert (constant chase `[0,0,3]`, FOV pinned to base). Information
  preserved by the existing instant-swap.
