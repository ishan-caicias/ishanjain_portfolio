## Continuity & attention check

- One primary motion per beat throughout; the drift/settle holds prevent double punctuation. ✓
- C² rotation ⇒ nothing for the chase-look damper to fight; E2E asserts the camera quaternion
  rate stays smooth (via the existing chase-camera spec, which passed). ✓
- Reduced motion: floor exempt, instant swap, no puffs, no envelope — information preserved. ✓
- Frame budget: scalar math + ≤20 ember spawns per journey; no allocation in the loop. ✓

## Ledger requests (for Astra)

The ≥1.5 s flip rides the existing seconds-scale journey license; **L15's magnitude numbers must
be re-stated** under the new duration table at the post-D3 REALISM-AUDIT (after D3.3). The v4
coast itself is a realism _improvement_ (constant-velocity coast is §3.2's own physics) — no new
license expected there.

## Owner-eyes checklist (the preview pass for TR-094's manual regression)

1. The flip reads as **three events** — engines die, ship turns, engines relight forward — not one blur.
2. The rotation is a deliberate arc — gentle start, commit, gentle landing; no snap at either end.
3. **RCS puffs read as thrusters**, not sparks/fire — if they read hot, say so (whiteness fallback ready).
4. During the flip **the sky does NOT slow down** — streaks/starfield hold speed (the coast).
5. After relight the plume visibly points at the destination.
6. **Home journey pacing:** does ~2.7 s still feel snappy? This is the slice's one open taste question.
7. Reduced motion: still the instant swap, no regression.

**Bottom line:** every beat of the brief shipped as designed within ADR-0011's corrected
mechanism; the two open items are owner-taste (pacing) and owner-look (puff tint), both with
one-line tuning paths ready.
