## 1. What this beat must communicate

**"The engines have stopped; the ship is turning around; now it is braking."** Three distinct
statements, in strict order, each with its own cue. Today all three are compressed into
84–252 ms — below the ~1 s threshold where rotation reads as a _cut_, not a manoeuvre — and on
a slow frame the beat vanishes entirely (TR-081). The floor (ADR-0011) buys ≥ 1.5 s of screen
time; this brief spends it.

The flip is the **narrative centrepiece of every journey** — the one moment the visitor sees
the ship _act_. It deserves the anticipation-commit-settle grammar: a beat of stillness after
the burn dies, the committed rotation with a clear arc, a beat of stillness before the retro
burn lights.

## 2. Beat sheet

k-values on the fixed window `[KA=0.44, KD=0.56]`; wall-clock columns at the 1,500 ms floor
(every current destination) — sub-beats scale linearly with the window.

| Beat                  | k range         | Wall-clock (floor)     | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Cue                                                                                                           |
| --------------------- | --------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1 · Burn cutoff**   | 0.440 → 0.452   | 0 → 150 ms             | Main plume collapses: burn envelope 1→0, eased (`smoothstep`). Streaks/β/FOV **hold peak** (the v4 coast — engines off ≠ slowing).                                                                                                                                                                  | Plume dies; HUD → `FLIP & BURN — ROTATING SHIP`                                                               |
| **2 · Drift beat**    | 0.452 → 0.465   | 150 → 310 ms           | Nothing moves but the stars. The anticipation hold — lets the cutoff register before the rotation begins.                                                                                                                                                                                           | Silence (visual)                                                                                              |
| **3 · Rotation**      | 0.465 → 0.545   | 310 → 1,310 ms         | Hull rotates 180° about its **local pitch (transverse) axis** on a C² `smootherstep` — zero angular velocity _and_ acceleration at both ends. ~1.0 s of committed arc at the floor. **RCS puff pair at rotation start; counter-pair at rotation end** (the thrusters that start and stop the spin). | The manoeuvre itself                                                                                          |
| **4 · Settle beat**   | 0.545 → 0.560   | 1,310 → 1,500 ms       | Stern now faces travel. Still. The settle before recommitment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Stillness again                                                                                               |
| **5 · Retro relight** | 0.560 → 0.560+δ | 0 → ~300 ms into decel | Burn envelope 0→1 eased over ~0.3 s. The plume is hull-attached and the hull has flipped, so thrust **visibly opposes motion** — no re-aim logic, geometry does it.                                                                                                                                 | Plume relights _toward_ the destination; HUD → `BRAKING BURN`; β/FOV begin their decay (u leaves the plateau) |

**Why rotation is [0.465, 0.545], not the full window:** the drift/settle beats are what make
the cutoff and relight readable as _separate events_ — back-to-back state changes are double
punctuation. 1.0 s of rotation is comfortably above the ~1 s cut-threshold at the floor.

## 3. Curves & constants (ready to implement)

```
// babylon-ship.ts — window widening (D3.2 step 1; wphase follows automatically per D3.1):
WARP_ACCEL_END   0.47 → 0.44
WARP_DECEL_START 0.53 → 0.56

// flipPhase(k): C² smootherstep across the ROTATION sub-window, not the full flip window:
FLIP_ROT_START = 0.465, FLIP_ROT_END = 0.545
t = clamp((k − FLIP_ROT_START) / (FLIP_ROT_END − FLIP_ROT_START), 0, 1)
flipPhase = t³·(t·(6t − 15) + 10)          // smootherstep: f'(0)=f'(1)=f''(0)=f''(1)=0

// Burn envelope (new, threaded through PlumeParams ADDITIVELY — absent field = today's booleans):
CUTOFF:  k ∈ [0.44, 0.452]  → env: 1→0, smoothstep
COAST:   k ∈ [0.452, 0.56]  → env: 0
RELIGHT: k ∈ [0.56, 0.56+δ] → env: 0→1, smoothstep,
         δ = min(0.3 s expressed in decel-k (= 0.3/warpDur_s · 0.44/0.44), 0.08)
         // capped: at short durations the shoulder compresses (~110 ms at home) rather than
         // eating the brake — a fast relight on a short hop reads fine; a long one reads late.
env multiplies the burning-phase plume params (alpha 0.9, throttle 0.85, flare 0.55) toward
their coasting values (0.15 / 0.06 / 0.07) — one scalar, all three cues agree.

// RCS puffs (reuse the ember buffer — MAX_EMBERS 48, one mesh, no new material):
PUFF_COUNT = 5 per burst, 4 bursts (rotation start: ±local-Y pair; rotation end: counter pair)
PUFF_SPEED ≈ 0.25 (unit-ship space, lateral), PUFF_LIFE ≈ 0.45 s, skip under reduced motion.
Placement: fore/aft hull flanks (±Z ends, ±Y faces) — the physically-correct couples for a
pitch rotation. If the ember's diesel-orange tint reads as fire rather than cold gas at
IMPLEMENT, a per-spawn whiteness parameter is the fallback (Procyon weighs vs bundle rules).
```

**Camera: explicitly no new motion.** The chase-look damping already keeps the frame steady
while the hull rotates within it — that _is_ the reference shot (§3.2: "the camera does NOT
mirror the hull's rotation"). The dilated window means the existing 0.35→0.55 chase-waypoint
pan slows to a crawl during the flip — desirable, camera near-holds; the 0.55 row stays where
it is (nudging it to the window edge would couple the tables for no visible gain). E2E asserts
camera quaternion rate stays smooth across the window (no spike at cutoff/relight).

## 4. Reduced motion & tiers

- **Reduced motion:** unchanged from today — instant hull swap at k>0.5, no rotation animation,
  no puffs, no envelope (booleans as-is), **no floor** (ADR-0011; the 350 ms profile stands).
  The information (ship now faces backwards, braking) survives via the swap + HUD label.
- **Tiers:** everything here is per-frame scalar math + ≤ 20 extra ember spawns per journey —
  free on every tier including WebGL2 fallback. No tier variants needed.

## 5. Attention & continuity checks

- One primary motion per beat: cutoff (plume), rotation (hull), relight (plume) — never two at
  once; the drift/settle holds enforce it.
- C² rotation ⇒ no angular-velocity step for the chase-look damper to fight; the E2E
  quaternion-rate assertion is the regression guard.
- The v4 coast holds β/FOV/streaks at peak through beats 1–4 (constant velocity — honest), so
  the _only_ things changing during the flip are the things the narrative is about.
- Cue-agreement with D3.1: the chase pull-back begins at k≈0.55–0.72 — i.e. the camera starts
  easing back just as the retro burn lights. Sequenced, not simultaneous: relight (0.56) then
  pull-back peak (0.72). ✓

## 6. Ledger requests (for Astra)

The ≥1.5 s flip is itself a **compression** of the real tens-of-seconds manoeuvre (§3.2) — this
rides the existing seconds-scale journey license (L15), whose magnitude numbers change under
ADR-0011's duration table; the post-D3 REALISM-AUDIT re-states L15. No other departures: the
coast plateau, thrust reversal, and RCS couples are all _more_ honest than the shipped triangle.

## 7. Owner-eyes checklist (after the D3.2 build)

1. The flip reads as three events — engines die, ship turns, engines relight pointing forward —
   not one blur. Each beat distinguishable at a glance.
2. The rotation is a deliberate arc: starts gently, commits, lands gently. No snap at either end.
3. RCS puffs read as "thrusters turning the ship", not sparks or damage.
4. During the flip the sky does NOT slow down (streaks/starfield hold speed — the coast).
5. After relight, the plume visibly points at the destination.
6. Home journey (goHome): the ~2.7 s total still feels snappy enough — **this is the pacing
   change ADR-0011 flags; say if 1.5 s is too long here and we tune `FLIP_MIN_MS`.**
7. Reduced motion: still the instant swap, no regression.
