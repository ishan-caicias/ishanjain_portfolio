   owner may re-wordsmith), chips, CTAs. The Sydney badge hides in travel mode (HUD already

3. **Engine glow on the hull** — `uRearDir`/`uEngineGlow` uniforms; warm light on rear-facing
   normals scaled by plume phase (`engineGlowIntensity`: burn 1.3 → coast 0.1). Visible in
   captures as the orange wash on the upper hull at idle and blazing tail during burns.

## F1 — Quaternion flight state

`ship-dynamics.ts` gains quaternion math (`quatFromUnitVectors` incl. antipodal case,
`quatSlerp`, `quatDamp`, `quatToMat4`, `restPoseQuat`). The engine's scripted
pitch/flip matrices are **gone**: `_shipQuat` slerp-damps (λ = 4.5) toward the **real travel
vector transformed into view space** during warp — nose along the route on acceleration,
retrograde after the midpoint (the flip now _emerges_ from the damping instead of being
keyframed) — and settles to the rest pose otherwise. Banking still layers on top; free-look
drag now orbits a ship that keeps pointing where it is genuinely going. Reduced motion snaps.
Capture proof: mid-warp the ship dives nose-first into the trail convergence point, engines
toward camera.
