## What this slice is

The visible sky is a set of nested backdrops (Astra §1), and two of them must now fade by the
**destination's distance** or they are broken physics:

- **D2.1 — solar-system furniture.** The asteroid belt (154,662 specks), its Havok physics, and the
  planet/glare billboards are present in the solar system and GONE past ~0.1 ly. Astra §2: the whole
  6.6-AU belt subtends **16 milli-arcsec** from M42 — a belt pixel there is wrong by ~7 orders of
  magnitude. The audited baseline drew the belt as a faint ring at _every_ destination.
- **D2.2 — the extragalactic collapse.** At an SDSS/NBG arrival (≥ 1e6 ly) the entire local Milky
  Way — the 360° band, the 168,959-star field, the constellation figures — fades out, and toward
  home a single external-galaxy **impostor** appears (Astra §1: a 30-kpc disc subtends ~10′ from
  32.6 Mly). Keeping a 360° band at a galaxy is the tripwire the brief names.

## What shipped

**Shared mechanism — per-mesh `uLayerFade` via material clones (WebGPU-safe).** The `ijStar`
fragment twins (GLSL + WGSL, line-parallel) gain a `uLayerFade` multiply on the final alpha, and the
uniform joins the star material's list. The main star field gets its own clone `_localMat` and the
belt its own clone `_beltMat`; **SDSS stays on the base `_starMat` at fade 1** (Astra §1: isotropic,
effectively infinite — never fades). Clones share the compiled program but own their UBO, which is
why this works on WebGPU where a single shared material's UBO uploads once (an `onBindObservable`
per-mesh set would give every mesh the last value). The clones are kept in lockstep with the base on
every per-frame uniform: `uTime` (belt orbital clock), aberration/Doppler (`_pushAberration`'s list),
`uViewport` (resize), `uHaloAmp` (tier change).

**D2.1 — furniture fade + Havok sleep.** New pure thresholds + schedulers in `ship-dynamics.ts`:
`FURNITURE_FADE_START_LY = 0.001`, `FURNITURE_GONE_LY = 0.1`, `furnitureVisibility(ly)`,
`localFieldVisibility(ly)`, and `frameLadderFade(from, to, k, reduced)` — a leaving layer fades out
over the accel phase (gone before the k≈0.5 flip), a returning layer fades in over decel; reduced
motion holds and swaps at arrival. `_updateFrameLadder()` (called each frame after `_tickWarp`)
drives `_beltMat.uLayerFade` from the destination `lyTotal` + warp `prog`, captured per journey in
`_beginWarp`. When the belt has faded out, `_tickAsteroids` skips the force loop and the collision
callback is gated on `_beltPhysicsAwake` — no phantom camera shake from invisible rocks.

**D2.2 — collapse + impostor.** `_localMat.uLayerFade`, the band's `uFade` (now
`_bandFadeAmt × _localFieldFade`, applied every frame in `_tickMilkyWay` except during ascent), and
the constellation `uColor` alpha (× `_localFieldFade` in `_pushAberration`) all collapse together at
≥ 1e6 ly. A new procedural, **license-clean** external-galaxy impostor (`milkyWayImpostorPixel` /
`buildMilkyWayImpostor` — an inclined disc from the band's own tone model, NOT the unverified-licence
Risinger pano) renders on a camera-facing quad with its own `ijImpostor` shader twins; it appears
astern of the extragalactic target, sized by the target's real angular subtense (`θ = 2·atan(15 kpc
/ d)`) floored to a visible minimum (declared license). Texture bound synchronously at creation (#9);
mesh hidden until an extragalactic arrival. A HUD legibility line
`LOCAL FIELD BELOW RESOLUTION · MILKY WAY ASTERN` shows at extragalactic arrival (derived in
`SpaceScene` from `arrivedId` + catalog `ly`) so the collapse reads as physics, not a bug.

New `sceneStats()` diagnostics: `furnitureFade`, `localFieldFade`, `beltPhysicsAwake`,
`impostorVisible`, `impostorTextureReady`; `bandFade` now reports the EFFECTIVE band opacity.

## Non-negotiables honoured

- **#4/#5 shader twins:** `uLayerFade` and the impostor sampler land in GLSL _and_ WGSL,
  line-parallel, identifier-identical; a unit test pins both source strings and guards the impostor
  WGSL against the reserved-identifier list (TR-045). `uLayerFade` is not reserved.
- **#6 async WGSL validation:** the impostor WGSL only compiles/draws after an extragalactic hop, so
  a hop + `impostorVisible` + zero-console assertion was added to `webgpu-hardware.spec` (fails-open
  without a real GPU). The frame-ladder spec's own zero-console-error assertion covers the GLSL path.
- **#9 sampler bind:** the impostor texture is a `RawTexture` built and bound at material creation,
  before the mesh (hidden at boot) can ever draw.
- **#1/#2 bundle:** subpath imports only; the impostor uses a bespoke `ShaderMaterial`, deliberately
  NOT `StandardMaterial` (which would have pulled a large side-effect module). JS +5 KB gz.
- **#18 behaviour, not readiness:** the E2E drives real `travelTo`/`goHome` and reads fades.
- **#24 reduced motion:** `frameLadderFade` holds and swaps at arrival — no animated fade.
