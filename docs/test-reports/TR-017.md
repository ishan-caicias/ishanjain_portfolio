## What Was Delivered

1. **`src/lib/ship-dynamics.ts`** — pure, unit-tested flight math: damped-spring integrator
   (semi-implicit Euler, clamped dt), NDC→view-space conversion, and the legacy-parity constants
   that reproduce the P1 screen composition exactly (`SHIP_NDC_Y_OFFSET`, `shipScaleFactor`).
2. **`_drawShip` placement rewritten** (both craft and wireframe paths): the clip-space
   post-shift chain (`persp(30°)` + NDC shift) is gone. The ship is now a view-space object at
   `SHIP_VIEW_DEPTH = 3` units in front of the scene camera, rendered under the **scene's own
   projection** (`70°·(1+warpSpeed·0.004)`) — so warp speed makes the ship breathe with the
   camera (fix #1).
3. **Mass dynamics** (fix #3): station glide is now an under-damped spring (ω = 3.2, ζ = 0.72 —
   overshoot + settle), with **look-lag** (the ship trails view-rotation velocity and springs
   back) and lateral-velocity banking folded into the existing bank term. Reduced motion snaps
   directly, as before.
4. **Tests** — +8 unit (spring convergence/overshoot/critical damping/dt-stability; NDC↔view
   round-trip through the engine's projection convention; two legacy-parity proofs), +1 E2E
   (full warp travel with the craft active).

`ship.{x,y,s,a}` semantics are preserved (same units/targets — only the trajectory shape and
the projection changed); no external readers existed regardless.

## Notes

  look; at 3 view units under the scene's 70° camera the nose foreshortens toward the viewer.

  constant, scale auto-compensates via `shipScaleFactor`).
- Spring/lag tuning constants are all in `ship-dynamics.ts` for P3 iteration.
