# TR-026 — PF-08 F3: Exhaust Realism (Layered Noise Plume + Embers)

**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Scope:** [PF-08-flight-v3.md](../delivery-plan/PF-08-flight-v3.md) Phase F3 — the final PF-08
item. Replace the flat additive cone plume with a layered, turbulent, propulsion-reading exhaust.
**Verdict: OVERALL: READY TO PROCEED**

## Scope note (proportionate to the imminent Babylon migration)

The graphics-engine evaluation ([architecture](../architecture/2026-07-18-graphics-physics-engine-evaluation.md))
selected a Babylon+WebGPU rebuild (PF-09) that replaces this plume with a GPU particle system.
F3 was therefore **right-sized**: the core visual wins were delivered; the one item that would be
throwaway screen-space work — a full **refraction heat-shimmer post-pass** — was implemented
instead as a lightweight in-plume two-tap noise interference, with the true refraction pass
explicitly deferred to the Babylon rebuild (where it is a built-in post-process). Recorded as a
deliberate deferral, not a gap.

## What changed

1. **Layered noise flame** — the plume vertex now carries an across-axis `side` coordinate
   (`PLUME_VERTEX_FLOATS` 4 → 5); a generated 128² fbm turbulence texture (`_buildPlumeNoise`,
   same procedural approach as the Milky Way band, zero new deps) is scrolled along the plume
   axis in the fragment shader. Tight white-hot **core** (`PLUME_CORE`) layered over a wide
   diesel-orange **sheath** (`PLUME_SHEATH`).
2. **Acceleration/throttle-driven turbulence** — new `plumeThrottle` (0..1, flickers during burn,
   steady in reduced motion) feeds `uThrottle`: noise eats further into the plume and brightness
   rises under thrust, so burn / coast / brake are visually distinct.
3. **Heat-shimmer (lightweight)** — two noise taps at different scroll rates interfere, giving a
   shimmer without a screen-space pass. Full refraction deferred to PF-09.
4. **Ember sparks** — `stepEmber` (pure, tested) + an engine burst of 8–14 point sprites on
   **burn start/stop**, drifting off the nozzles and fading through three life buckets (no pop),
   capped at 48, reusing the round-point FLAT shader. Reduced motion: no embers.
5. Context-restore safe (`_buildPlumeNoise` + `aUV` re-grabbed in `_rebuildGL`); reduced-motion
   and wireframe/craft paths unchanged.
