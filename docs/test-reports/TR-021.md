# TR-021 — Craft Shader Hotfix: Bleached-White Hull

**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Scope:** Owner-reported visual regression — "ship has lost all its textures, seems opaque white"
**Verdict: OVERALL: READY TO PROCEED**

## Diagnosis (measured, not guessed)

The hull texture was never white: channel means measured **71/76/79 (of 255)** — a dark
blue-gray. The P1/P3 shader lit **sRGB-encoded** samples directly and then applied
`col/(col+0.55)*1.5`, a curve that maps the whole 0.3–0.8 midtone band into 0.63–0.95 and
clips highlights at 1.0. At the P5 2.5× hero scale that bleaching filled the screen: contrast
crushed, panel detail gone, "opaque white". A textbook missing-sRGB-pipeline defect — the
2.5× scale exposed it, it didn't cause it.

## Fix

`CRAFT_FS` now runs a proper-ish pipeline: **decode sRGB → light in linear → Reinhard
(asymptotic, never clips) → encode sRGB** (`x²` / `sqrt` approximations, cheap in mediump).
Lighting energy retuned for linear space; emissive linearized (`uEmiBoost` 1.55 → 2.0); rim
folded into linear at 0.55. Verified by capture: warm gold-brown wing tones, gray panel
structure, and dark underbody are back; no clipped whites.
