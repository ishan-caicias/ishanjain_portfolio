## Step 3 — impact camera shake

- Every Havok body gets `setCollisionCallbackEnabled(true)` + a collision observer. Amplitude
  derives from the **solver's own impulse** (`IPhysicsCollisionEvent.impulse`) with
  inverse-square distance falloff to the camera (`impactShakeAmplitude`, pure): a heavy nearby
  crash thumps, a distant graze whispers, micro-contacts below `minImpulse` are ignored.
- The ring-down (`shakeOffset`, two incommensurate sine channels per axis — chaotic to the eye,
  deterministic to the tests) applies to the **camera object only**: `this.cam` — the canonical
  position the arrival E2E asserts exactly-on-target — is never polluted. Exponential decay via
  the shared `expDamp`.
- **Reduced motion: no shake at all** (the observer returns before accumulating).

## Step 4 — docking contact

The plan's "gentle constraint/impulse as the ship berths", implemented as impulse + contact
spring rather than a full Havok constraint pair (a body-per-station physics rig for a 1.65 s
berth beat fails the proportionality test; recorded as a deliberate scope decision):

- At arrival the shake system receives a soft **berthing bump** (`DOCK_CONTACT.bumpAmp` 0.32).
- The hull **overshoots into the berth and rings down** along its approach axis
  (`dockSettleOffset`: damped sinusoid, 0 at contact, < 1% after ~5τ — unit-pinned), riding the
  existing dock-hold window before the dossier handoff fade.
- Reduced motion: neither bump nor settle.
